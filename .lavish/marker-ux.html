UX
Markers sit at 12:00 above the exhibit by default (world-relative). When panning, each marker drifts in the opposite direction (~28% of pan, max 42 px), revealing the exhibit footprint underneath. Drift decays back when panning stops.
UX
While drifted, a dashed caret line connects the marker to a small geo-anchor dot at the exact coordinate — so the viewer always knows which exhibit the marker belongs to.
