# Storylines

## → A. The QR Door *(building this)*
Form: Object-to-object chain. Device: The feature as hook.
Opens on blurred warm park gradient; a QR code marker materialises; scan brackets lock; each app screen world follows one through the next. Viewer feels: mystery → recognition → discovery.
First 2 s: blurred park fill + "Where do you start?" slams in at scale.
Beats: problem title → QR scan locks → audio screen → map screen → gamification → three-phone fan → coming soon.
Look: warm paper and deep forest; Outfit 900 at scale; no glow, no particles.
Sound: no voice; music bed placeholder — warm percussion + strings.
*Recommended: tightest message, single visual trick carried through every scene.*

---

## B. Lost Visitor
Form: Match cuts. Device: A moment in someone's day.
A visitor stares at a kiosk map → phone lights up → match cuts carry them exhibit to exhibit. More human warmth, less kinetic impact. Best when real park footage is available.

## C. Beat Drop
Form: Cut on the beat. Device: Type carries the argument.
Giant kinetic type on flat colour fields: "SCAN." "LISTEN." "EXPLORE." Each word splits into the app screen it names. Maximum energy; highest execution complexity; needs a music track with a clear beat grid.
